# Reviewer and Deadlines

> **Example data.** Replace the bracketed names with your team's real people before building.

## People

| Role | Person | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T6 reviewer | [Reviewer name] | Reviews every proposal packet and records Ready for release, Needs revision, or Needs clarification |
| Backup reviewer | [Backup name] | Reviews when the T6 reviewer is unavailable for more than 1 business day |
| Event coordinator | [Coordinator name] | Receives exceptions, tool failures, and packets that reach a third Needs revision |
| Client-facing employee | [Employee name] | Sends clarification requests and final proposals, and enters client replies under the inquiry ID |

## Time limits

| Item | Limit | What happens when it is reached |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T6 human review | 10 minutes per packet | The reviewer records an outcome; unfinished reviews are flagged on the dashboard |
| T7 revision agent | 10 minutes and 8 tool calls per run | T7 hands the packet back to the T6 reviewer |
| Revision rounds | 2 per inquiry ID | A third Needs revision goes to the event coordinator |
| Client clarification reply | 3 business days (Monday–Friday, excluding holidays) | Run is marked Stopped – no response |
| Clarification reminder | 1 reminder on business day 2 | Sent by the client-facing employee |
| Whole workflow (employee time) | 30 minutes per proposal (target; currently 1 hour) | Times above target are reviewed weekly |

## Target time budget (employee minutes per proposal)

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Paste inquiry and check extracted fields (T1) | 5 minutes |
| Check setup list (T3) | 5 minutes |
| Review packet (T6) | 10 minutes |
| One revision check, if needed (T6 after T7) | 5 minutes |
| Add client details and send (release) | 5 minutes |
| **Total** | **30 minutes** |
